COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you come

Is my visit really confidential?

Completely. Records are protected by law, rooms are enclosed, and nothing is shared without your consent.

Will I definitely be put on medicines?

No. Sometimes it's counselling, sometimes lifestyle changes, sometimes medicines — decided with you, openly.

Can I consult online?

The first visit is in person; after that, follow-ups can happen by private video call with prescriptions on your phone.

What if someone is in crisis right now?

Go to the nearest hospital casualty now. For urgent emotional support call Tele-MANAS 14416 — free, 24×7, Tamil & English.
